# Recurring Symbolism Across the Career

How the same images evolve across 12 albums (2006 → 2025). Per-album details are in [`docs/data/eras.json`](../docs/data/eras.json).

> No lyrics are quoted in this repo. Symbols are described in our own words and linked to song titles.

| Symbol | First seen | How it evolves | Peak appearances |

| **The number 13** | Debut era (written on her hand on tour) | Lucky charm → structural device (Midnights is 13 tracks about 13 sleepless nights) → release-time puzzles | Fearless tour, Midnights |
| **Color as era code** | Fearless gold | Every era gets a palette, so fans read new colors as announcements (Showgirl's orange countdown) | Red, 1989, Showgirl |
| **Rain** | Debut / Fearless | Romantic backdrop → loneliness → cleansing and recovery | "Fearless", "Clean", "Midnight Rain" |

## Through-lines by career phase

1. **Country storyteller (2006–2010).** Diary-style songs, names as easter eggs, and a fairy-tale lens.
2. **Pop transition (2012–2014).** Seasons, colors, and cities become shorthand. The media's image of her becomes material she satirizes.
3. **Reclamation (2017–2019).** Symbols get weaponized and then healed (snake → butterfly). Privacy becomes a theme.
4. **Fiction era (2020).** Characters and other people's stories, with ghosts and nature.
5. **Self-mythology (2022–2025).** Songs comment on her own legend: masterminds, tortured poets, and showgirls. Recurring images get reused on purpose as callbacks.
